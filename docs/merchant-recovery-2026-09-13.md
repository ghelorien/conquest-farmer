# Merchant reconnect and booth recovery fixes

Reconnect now keeps its input lease separate from ordinary booth work, waits for
the real game window instead of the loading splash, and launches the installed
client only when its fingerprint matches. A dead embedded window is detached
without turning a reconnect into a manual pause. Known login sessions can be
reattached by their exact saved process identity. Focus recovery uses the verified
Conquest title bar when normal Windows foreground activation fails.

Travel retries short-lived torn life/scene reads and camera changes that happen
before mouse-down. It never repeats an uncertain submitted movement or fare.
Conductress clicks wait for the native scene pointer. Outside the eight-tile
vacancy observation radius, recovery approaches the saved Market area first.

A vacant flag uses its native collision box, followed by the native Open Booth
confirmation. The confirmation must contain the selected flag UID in the pinned
callback and expose the expected Yes control under the pointer. Confirmation
intent is journaled before input. Booth ownership, exact stock and silver are
reconciled before opening the separate booth panel and restoring saved listings.

## Live evidence and remaining qualification

Dutch logged in using its saved encrypted account. The Conductress transfer was
completed manually by the user, so this recovery is assisted and does not qualify
full unattended reconnect. The subsequent Market approach, vacant flag click,
Open Booth confirmation and owned-panel opening were automatic. Exact stock was
checked against the pre-disconnect inventory and booth. Listings restore at their
previous verified asking prices, highest value first.

Assisted trials retain their intervention record and cannot set
`automatic_recovery_verified`. Full automatic Conductress recovery and the
remaining merchant delivery qualification still require live validation; the
global delivery rollout gate remains in place. Private credentials, memory
samples and transaction journals are excluded from publication.
